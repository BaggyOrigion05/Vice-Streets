#Vice Streets
Single player free throw range shooting
Player must make 5 free throw shots within 5 levels
The game levels increase in difficulty as each new level is unlocked
Game characters are unlocked on each lnew level
The net moves horizontally back and forth
Each level represents a faster horizontal shifting of the net
For shooting, the free throw shot must be put on hold when applying the shoot button
A shooting bar/meter at the bottom of the player will be displayed to help you measure the shot
Too much or less power applied on the shot will minimize chances of perfect execution/netting
